AudioSync has no account, no analytics and no crash reporter.

It plays audio only on the speakers you turn on. It looks for network speakers on your own network, and the Web Remote stays off until you turn it on.

The one request it makes past your network on its own is the update check: at launch and once a day it reads the release list on GitHub to see whether a newer version is out. Nothing downloads until you say yes. To turn the check off, open AudioSync and choose File > Check for Updates Automatically.

Your settings and speaker pairings stay on this computer, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AudioSync.

The full privacy note: https://github.com/yassersutojoyo/audiosync/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
